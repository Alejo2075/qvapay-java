# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a respectful, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about the work, not the person.
- Respect other people's time: keep issues and pull requests focused.
- Protect privacy: never post other people's personal data, credentials or financial information.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or unwanted attention.
- Soliciting or sharing credentials, or asking others to perform payments on your behalf.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact options on their GitHub profile (<https://github.com/Alejo2075>) or the repository's private security reporting. Reports are handled confidentially.

This code applies in all project spaces and when someone represents the project in public. It is a short, original text inspired by widely used community standards such as the Contributor Covenant.
